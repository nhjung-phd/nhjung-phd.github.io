Research Portfolio

My research examines how artificial intelligence, data-driven methods, and operations research can support decision making in financial, organizational, and operational environments. The portfolio is organized around three primary research programs, complemented by emerging streams in AI–finance, geoeconomics, and trustworthy AI.

This page presents a public-facing overview. Detailed hypotheses, proprietary research designs, and unpublished methodological mechanisms are intentionally omitted.

Core Research Programs

RP1

AI Decision Intelligence

AI-assisted decision support and decision making under uncertainty, with a focus on financial allocation, reinforcement learning, large language models, and policy design.

Themes

  • Asset allocation and portfolio decisions
  • Deep reinforcement learning
  • LLM-based decision support
  • AI-assisted policy design
  • Human–AI decision systems
RP2

Financial Operations & Decision Systems

Decision integrity and operational coordination in complex financial systems, combining operations research, simulation, distributed systems, and domain knowledge from financial-market operations.

Themes

  • Trading and order-management systems
  • Resource coordination and concurrency
  • Distributed decision systems
  • Simulation and operations research
  • Financial-process design
RP3

Digital Organization & International Business

How digital technologies, AI-enabled organizational structures, and transnational consumer activity reshape strategy, internationalization, work, and governance.

Themes

  • Digital internationalization
  • AI decision delegation
  • AI governance
  • Global strategy
  • Future of work and workation

Emerging & Complementary Streams

RP4

AI–Finance Structural Dynamics

Structural interactions among AI investment, financing, market valuation, and the broader financial system.

RP5

Geoeconomics & Commodity Markets

Geopolitical shocks, sanctions, exchange rates, critical minerals, and structural changes in commodity and financial markets.

RP6

Trustworthy AI for Decision Making

Reliability, explainability, calibration, grounded evidence, and robustness in AI-supported decisions.

RP5 · Geoeconomics & Commodity Markets

Current research examines structural changes in the oil–exchange-rate relationship surrounding the Russia–Ukraine War and extends toward geopolitical shocks, critical minerals, and commodity-market dynamics.

RP6 · Trustworthy AI for Decision Making

Current work focuses on the reliability and grounded alignment of AI-generated explanations in decision-support settings, including prediction–explanation consistency, robustness, and calibration.
